Terms of Service

VISIQ is a free static science simulation site. These terms are short on purpose. If anything here is unclear, ask.

1. What this site is

VISIQ is a gallery of interactive science simulations you run in your browser. It is a static site: nothing you do here is sent to a server we operate, and we do not run a database. The simulations are for personal curiosity and learning. They are not professional engineering or scientific instruments, and we do not guarantee that any number shown is suitable for real-world design or measurement.

2. What we store

Everything the site remembers is kept in your browser's localStorage, on your device only: your theme choice, audio mute state, favorited simulations, and any drawn paths or pinned controls you create inside a simulation. None of it leaves your device. We cannot see it, export it, or delete it for you — clearing your browser data removes it.

There is an optional login that stores an email and password in your browser's localStorage. Passwords are stored in plaintext, which is a known defect. The login only controls whether the gallery welcome screen shows; every simulation opens without it. Do not reuse a password you care about.

3. What we do not do

We do not run analytics, telemetry, or fingerprinting. We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect it. We do not offer cloud saving, leaderboards, shared scenes, or accounts that follow you between devices. If any of those are added later, these terms will be rewritten before they ship.

4. Third-party requests

The page loads p5.js from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. That is the only request to a third-party host. Share buttons open Twitter or Reddit in a new tab only when you click them; they do not send anything until you do.

5. Your conduct

Use the simulations however you like. Do not try to break the site, scrape it at scale, or pretend it is something it is not. We reserve the right to block traffic that harms the site for other visitors.

6. No warranty

The site and simulations are provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. We do not guarantee uptime, accuracy, or that a simulation will run on your device. Physics models simplify reality and each simulation's own "what this model leaves out" line states its limits honestly.
