Privacy Policy

VISIQ does not collect your personal data. This policy explains what is stored on your device, what is not, and the one third-party request the page makes.

1. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses (beyond what your internet provider and the host normally log), device identifiers, or usage data. There is no analytics script, no telemetry, and no fingerprinting.

2. What is stored on your device

The site writes to your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage only. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.

KeyWhat it holds
`visiq_theme`Your chosen theme (dark or light)
`visiq_muted` / `visiq_audio_muted`Whether audio is muted
`visiq-stats`Per-device simulation statistics history
`visiq_users`Emails and passwords for the optional local login (plaintext — a known defect)
`visiq_session`, `visiq_user_email`Your active local session, if you log in
`visiq-onboarding-dismissed`Whether you dismissed the onboarding tour
Per-sim keysPinned controls, drawn paths, streaks, achievements, preferences

Clearing your browser data (or using private/incognito mode) removes all of it. We cannot access, export, or delete it for you because it never leaves your device.

3. The optional login

You may create a local account with an email and password. It is stored in plaintext in your browser's localStorage — this is a known defect, documented in the repo audit. Do not reuse a password you use elsewhere. The login does not gate anything: every simulation and the gallery open without logging in.

4. Third-party requests

The page loads p5.js from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. cdnjs may log standard request information (such as your IP address and browser type) as part of serving the file. That is their practice, not ours; we have no access to their logs. This is the only request the page makes to a third-party host.

Share buttons (Twitter, Reddit) open in a new tab only when you click them. No data is sent until you do.

5. Children

VISIQ is aimed at curious people aged 15 to 80. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13, partly because we do not collect personal data at all.

6. Your rights

Since we do not collect or store your data on any server, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on your behalf. To remove what is stored on your device, clear your browser's site data for visiq.

7. Changes

This policy may change. The current version is always at this URL.

8. Contact

Questions: toibawani14@gmail.com. This is a student portfolio project, not a company, so there is no data-protection officer and no guaranteed response time.
